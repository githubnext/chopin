# JEV-003 — Useful questions/preferences unapplied; incomplete review recovery

Status: Queued; clarify product scope before implementation. PR: Pending.

## Problem and evidence

The combined message “Separate decision for this browser stress test: How should
we notify maintainers about critical backend errors? Slack and email are the two
notification channels I want us to consider.” was held with 45% new-thread
confidence despite strong question detection. A clearer follow-up created an
independent alert card.

“Email is the alternative alert-delivery option. It gives us a durable record,
but urgent messages could be missed in a busy inbox. I prefer Slack for critical
errors.” produced three “no useful role” excerpts. Review attached the email
tradeoff as a Reason; the preference remained unapplied. The held-question
Add-to-card form offered only Option, Reason and Constraint on existing cards,
with no way to create that question. See the
[live browser pass](../jev-live-browser-test.md).

## Concrete reproduction

1. Send the combined alert question/options message beside an existing monitoring
   decision. Inspect extraction, role and new-thread confidence/targeting.
2. Create the alert card with a clearer independent question.
3. Send the combined email tradeoff/Slack preference message; inspect held roles.
4. Use Review correction for the email reason and inspect preference recovery.
5. Review the held question and inspect creation/attachment choices.

## Expected behavior and provisional scope

Useful questions, tradeoffs and preferences should apply to the intended decision
or offer a deliberate recovery route. Investigate classifier/targeting policy;
do not simply lower every confidence threshold. Determine whether held-question
creation and preference recovery are independent product changes; if so, assign
new stable issue IDs and separate PRs before implementing them.

## Acceptance checks

- Record extraction/targeting evidence, distinguishing observations from policy
  hypotheses; cover independent questions and genuinely ambiguous messages.
- Clarify intended held-question and preference recovery behavior with Maggie.
- Preserve conservative targeting and prevent unrelated-card contamination.
- Deterministic policy/recovery tests plus actual-browser checks as applicable.
- Document and split independent recovery changes before implementation.
- Separate implementer, independent tester and reviewer for each resulting PR.
