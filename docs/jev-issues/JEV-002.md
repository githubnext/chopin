# JEV-002 — Stale rationale after changing a choice

Status: Queued. PR: Pending.

## Problem and evidence

Chat attributed exception grouping and stack traces to Sentry. Saving Sentry
produced matching prose. After reopening and selecting manually added
OpenTelemetry with Grafana, the replacement paragraph named Grafana but retained
that rationale without new Grafana evidence. Observed in the
[live browser pass](../jev-live-browser-test.md).

`apps/server/src/conversation-plan/prose-job.ts` filters reasons and passes the
previous paragraph to `prosePrompt()`. This is a candidate boundary, not a proven
root cause.

## Concrete reproduction

1. Propose Sentry with exception grouping and stack-trace rationale; save it.
2. Reopen and manually add/select OpenTelemetry with Grafana, with no rationale
   that attributes those capabilities to Grafana.
3. Save and inspect the replacement paragraph and its generation prompt.

## Expected behavior and scope

Ground prose in the current choice and applicable sourced reasons. Replace prior
prose without copying unsupported reasons belonging to the previous option.
Scope is reason provenance and prose prompt/generation; anchor lifecycle belongs
to JEV-001, and extraction/review policy to JEV-003.

## Acceptance checks

- Deterministic provenance/prompt coverage excludes old-option-only rationale.
- Supported shared/current-choice reasons remain available.
- Replacement produces one current-choice passage.
- Targeted browser/provider check where practical, with model limits documented.
- Separate implementer, independent tester and reviewer; individual fix PR.

## Implementation, verification and limits

Queued. Candidate boundary only; provider quality and first cause unconfirmed.
